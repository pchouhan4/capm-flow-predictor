## Research integrity / methodology-focused angle (strong differentiator)

> Conducted a self-directed audit of a completed research pipeline, discovering and fixing a train/holdout data-leakage bug, a stale and incorrect headline statistic, and a flawed ancillary finding that didn't survive a properly-constructed replication — and documented every correction transparently rather than quietly revising the conclusion, producing a fully reproducible negative result.

## Shortest version (one line, for a dense bullet list)

> Audited and corrected a quant research pipeline for data leakage and statistical validity issues (Python/pandas/statsmodels), producing a reproducible, multiple-testing-corrected null finding on 1,800+ days of Indian equity data.

---

**Note on the "audit" framing:** this is the most differentiated angle if you're applying anywhere that values rigor over flashy results — most candidates show a model that "worked." Showing one that *didn't*, and proving it didn't through several rounds of self-correction, is a stronger signal of research maturity for quant/data-science interviews that probe methodology. Worth keeping the framing even if the specific wording above doesn't fit your CV's voice.
